    The loop

    One lap, four beats

    1. I

      Gather

      Project earnings are collected in one place instead of scattered across wallets.

    2. II

      Return

      The pot is aimed at the coin it came from, in daylight, along a trail anyone can trace.

    3. III

      Retire

      Supply the loop reclaims can leave circulation for good.

    4. IV

      Again

      A fresh lap begins from a leaner base, and the circuit closes on itself.
